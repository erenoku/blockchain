sCrypt 2-of-2 Payment Channels · BRC-100 Identity · BRC-29 Remittance

An open AI marketplace, paid word by word in real time.

Independent providers offer OpenAI and Google Gemini models at their own prices, and you choose who serves you. Lock a few satoshis once in a TokenChannel contract, then pay for each chunk the moment it arrives. No subscriptions, no credit balances, no wallet popup per prompt, and BSV network fees measured in fractions of a cent.

contract TokenChannel(PubKey customer, PubKey provider, int refundLockTime) SIGHASH_ALL | FORKID
01 ON-CHAIN · 1 TX

Lock 2-of-2 UTXO

Customer funds a 2-of-2 sCrypt contract output in BSV Desktop. An on-chain nLockTime branch guarantees a trustless refund if the provider goes offline.

02 OFF-CHAIN · SSE

Stream & Co-Sign Chunks

An ephemeral session key signs monotonically increasing stateSats commitments per streamed SSE chunk. Stop generation at any point and keep unstreamed funds.

03 BRC-29 · 0-CONF

Cooperative Settlement

Closing the channel broadcasts the final co-signed spend: earned satoshis arrive in the provider's BSV Desktop inbox and unused deposit returns to your wallet.
